Set up in six steps

Accept your invitation

Open the welcome email from HootOwl and click your personal link. Create a password of at least 12 characters. Link works for 7 days

Get your HootOwl number

In your workspace, open Plan & security. Enter the area code you'd like, tick the consent box, and click Set up my phone number.

Add the phone that should ring

Open Business. Under Forward incoming calls to, enter your cell number with country code, like +19405551234, and click Save number.

Meet your AI receptionist

Open AI receptionist. Name your assistant, write your greeting and closing line (ideas on the next page), list your services and area, and pick one of six voices. Click Save receptionist.

Hear it yourself

Click Call me to preview. We call your phone so you can talk to your assistant like a customer would. Tweak and preview again until it sounds right.

Go live

Tick My receptionist is ready — answer my calls with AI and save. That's it: your HootOwl number is now answered for you.

Make it sound like you

Greeting ideas

Your greeting is the first thing every caller hears. Keep it short and friendly, and say your business name.

"Thanks for calling Smith Plumbing! How can we help you today?"
"Hi, you've reached Lone Star Heating and Air. What can we do for you?"
"Hey, thanks for calling Red River Roofing. What's going on at your place today?"

Closing line ideas

Said after your assistant saves the caller's details. Only promise what you can deliver.

"Thanks! We've got your details, and someone from our team will call you back shortly."
"Perfect, you're all set. We'll call you back today to schedule your visit."

How your {{MINUTES}} AI minutes work

  • Your assistant answers calls to your HootOwl number while you have minutes left this month.
  • Each call uses its length in minutes, rounded up. A typical call takes 2 to 3 minutes, so {{MINUTES}} minutes covers {{CALLS}} calls.
  • Once they're used, calls ring your phone like normal call forwarding. Nobody hits a dead line.
  • Your minutes reset on the 1st of every month. You can see this month's minutes on your AI receptionist page.
  • Preview calls you make to yourself don't use your minutes.

Good to know

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. Your assistant is friendly and natural, but it never claims to be a person. If someone asks, it says it's your virtual assistant.

Does it text my customers?

No. HootOwl handles phone calls only and never sends texts to your callers.

Can it book appointments?

It collects each caller's name, address, service and problem and saves them as a lead, so you can call back and set the time.

What if someone has an emergency?

If a caller mentions danger such as fire, smoke, a gas smell or sparks, your assistant tells them to hang up and call 911 first.

How do I change my greeting or voice later?

Any time, on your AI receptionist page. Save, then use Call me to preview to hear the change.

Your minutes

How your 15 AI minutes work.

  • Your receptionist answers calls to your HootOwl number while you have minutes left this month.
  • A typical call takes 2 to 3 minutes, so 15 minutes covers about 5 to 7 calls.
  • Once they're used, calls ring your phone like normal call forwarding. Nobody hits a dead line.
  • Minutes reset on the 1st of every month, and calls you make to preview your receptionist are free.

Questions

Good to know.

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. Your receptionist is friendly and natural, but it never claims to be a person. If someone asks, it says it's your virtual assistant.

Does it text my customers?

No. HootOwl handles phone calls only and never sends text messages to your callers.

Can it schedule appointments?

It collects each caller's name, address, service and problem and saves it as a lead, so you or your team can call back and set the time.

Can I hear it before customers do?

Yes. From your workspace, press "Call me to preview" and your receptionist calls your phone so you can talk to it like a customer would.
